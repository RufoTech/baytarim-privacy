Baytarım — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · App: Baytarım (com.rainnovation.baytarim)
AZENRU

1. Who we are

Baytarım helps users in Baku find veterinary clinics, manage pet profiles, book visits and use SOS help. Operator: RA Innovations / Rufat Alakbarli. Contact: alekberovrufa@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email/password, Google Sign-In or phone (SMS) verification; name and profile photo.
  • Pets: name, type, breed, age and photos you upload.
  • Location: approximate/precise location when you allow it, to show nearby clinics.
  • Bookings & reviews, push tokens, favorites and SOS/widget settings.

3. How we use data

  • Maps, clinic discovery and navigation links
  • Accounts, pets and reservations
  • Push notifications and SOS / home-screen widget
  • Security, abuse prevention and service operation

4. Third parties

  • Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Messaging, Analytics)
  • Google Sign-In and Google Maps / Geolocation
  • API hosting, MongoDB, Redis cache, Socket.IO

5. Android permissions

  • Internet; fine/coarse location; notifications; camera/photos when adding a pet image.

6. Retention & rights

We keep data as needed to run the service. You can delete your account in-app or email us. You may request access, correction or deletion at the contact above. Not directed at children under 16.
